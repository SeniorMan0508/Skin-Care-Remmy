How Remi Luxe Beauty Began

Growing up, founder Remi recognized a deep void in the luxury skincare industry: too many prestigious brands relied on generic formulas, heavy perfumes, and synthetic fillers that aggravated sensitive and melanin-rich skin.

Driven by a passion for botanical alchemy and modern dermal science, Remi partnered with clean cosmetic chemists to formulate solutions that target real concerns—hyperpigmentation, compromised moisture barriers, uneven texture, and dehydration—without compromising on skin safety.

The result is Remi Luxe Beauty: an uncompromising collection of bio-compatible elixirs, velvet barrier creams, and pure 24K gold-infused oils that transform daily skincare into a moment of pure royalty.
